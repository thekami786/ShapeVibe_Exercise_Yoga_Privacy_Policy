# ShapeVibe Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use **ShapeVibe: Exercise & Yoga** (the App), hosted on GitHub or distributed as an Android mobile application on Google Play.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Storage

ShapeVibe is designed to give you complete control over your fitness journey while minimizing data collection:

* **Local Fitness & Progress Data**: Your workout progress, daily streak counter, completed exercise history, water tracker logs, estimated calories burned, fitness goals, body metrics (weight/height), and reminder preferences are stored locally on your device using local storage (`SharedPreferences`).
* **Account & Cloud Sync (Optional)**: If you choose to create an account or sign in with Google Sign-In or Email, basic profile information (display name, email address, avatar selection) and fitness statistics are securely processed and stored via **Google Firebase Authentication** and **Cloud Firestore**.
* **No Sensitive Medical / Location Tracking**: The App does not track your real-time GPS location, nor does it access sensitive health sensors or medical records.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The App integrates trusted third-party services that comply with Google Play Developer Policies:

* **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)**: We use Google Mobile Ads to deliver banner and interstitial advertisements to support free workouts. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers and anonymous usage signals in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
* **Google Firebase (Firebase Auth & Cloud Firestore)**: Used for secure authentication and cloud synchronization of your workout profile. Firebase data is protected with industry-standard TLS encryption.
* **Google Play Services**: Used to provide app distribution, integrity checks, and update notifications.

---

## 3. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In compliance with Google Play User Data and Account Deletion policies, you have the full right to delete your account and all associated data at any time:

* **In-App Deletion**: You can permanently delete your profile, streak records, workout logs, and cloud storage at any time by going to **Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Account & Data**. This permanently purges all records immediately.
* **Manual Deletion Request**: You may also email us at the contact address below with your registered account email to request complete manual deletion of all records within 48 hours.

---

## 4. Data Export & Portability

You retain ownership of your fitness logs. You can export a complete backup of all completed workout sessions and statistics in standardized JSON format directly from the app under **Profile → Privacy & Security → Export Workout Data**.

---

## 5. Device Permissions

The App requests only the minimal permissions required to function:
* `INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required for Firebase account sync and Google AdMob ad rendering.
* `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Optional permission on Android 13+ to deliver your custom daily workout reminders.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

ShapeVibe does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected, we will delete it immediately.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory updates. Any changes will be published here with an updated revision date.
