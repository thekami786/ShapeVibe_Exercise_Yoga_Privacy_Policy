# ShapeVibe Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use **ShapeVibe: Exercise & Yoga** (the "App"), hosted on GitHub and distributed as an Android mobile application on Google Play.

---

## 1. Information Collection & Storage

ShapeVibe is designed to give you complete control over your fitness journey while minimizing data collection:

* **Local Fitness & Progress Data:** Your workout progress, daily streak counter, completed exercise history, water tracker logs, estimated calories burned, fitness goals, body metrics (weight/height), and reminder preferences are stored locally on your device using local storage (`SharedPreferences`).
* **Account & Cloud Sync (Optional):** If you choose to create an account or sign in with Google Sign-In or Email, basic profile information (display name, email address, avatar selection) and fitness statistics are securely processed and stored via **Google Firebase Authentication** and **Cloud Firestore**.
* **No Sensitive Medical / Location Tracking:** The App does not track your real-time GPS location, nor does it access sensitive health sensors or medical records.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & SDKs

The App integrates trusted third-party services that comply with Google Play Developer Policies:

* **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** We use Google Mobile Ads to deliver banner and interstitial advertisements to support free workouts. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers and anonymous usage signals in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **Google Firebase (Firebase Auth & Cloud Firestore):** Used for secure authentication and cloud synchronization of your workout profile. Firebase data is protected with industry-standard TLS encryption.
* **Google Play Services:** Used to provide app distribution, integrity checks, and update notifications.

---

## 3. Data Retention Policy

We practice data minimization and retain your information only as long as necessary to provide you with the App's features:

* **Local Data Retention:** All locally stored workout logs, streak counters, water tracking history, and user settings are retained on your device for as long as the App is installed. You can delete this data anytime by clearing the App data via Android Settings or using the in-app deletion feature.
* **Account & Cloud Data Retention:** For users who create an account, your profile information (display name, email, avatar) and cloud-synced workout data are retained in Google Firebase only for as long as your account remains active.
* **Retention Period:** Personal data is not retained beyond the active lifetime of your account. If an account is inactive for more than 24 consecutive months, we reserve the right to securely purge inactive account data from our cloud databases.
* **Third-Party Telemetry Retention:** Third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob) retain technical telemetry and advertising identifiers in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 4. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In compliance with Google Play User Data and Account Deletion policies, you have the full right to delete your account and all associated data at any time:

* **In-App Immediate Deletion:** You can permanently delete your profile, streak records, workout logs, and cloud storage at any time by going to **Profile &rarr; Privacy & Security &rarr; Delete Account & Data**. This permanently purges all records immediately from both the device and cloud servers.
* **Manual Deletion Request:** You may also email us at [thekami786@gmail.com](mailto:thekami786@gmail.com) with your registered account email to request complete manual deletion of all records. Manual requests are processed and permanently purged within 48 hours.

---

## 5. Data Export & Portability

You retain ownership of your fitness logs. You can export a complete backup of all completed workout sessions and statistics in standardized JSON format directly from the app under **Profile &rarr; Privacy & Security &rarr; Export Workout Data**.

---

## 6. Device Permissions

The App requests only the minimal permissions required to function:

* `INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required for Firebase account sync and Google AdMob ad rendering.
* `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`: Optional permission on Android 13+ to deliver your custom daily workout reminders.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

ShapeVibe does not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected, we will delete it immediately.

---

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or regulatory updates. Any changes will be published here with an updated revision date.
